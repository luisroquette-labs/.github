## The quest

I'm Luis Roquette. At [CF Gauss](https://cfgauss.com.br), I build AI products and business workflows that can be tested, operated, and improved in the real world. Rocket Labs is where I share the open-source parts of that work.

The rule behind every build: **show the evidence, keep the failure visible, and give people a clear way to intervene.**

<summary><strong>Open the field notes</strong> · How I build</summary>

- **Model the task:** define the input, output, edge cases, and the point where a person decides.
- **Build the smallest working system:** reuse platform features and familiar tools.
- **Prove the behavior:** use tests, checks, and production evidence appropriate to the change.
- **Keep ownership clear:** visible status, recoverable failures, and human control.
